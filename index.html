Quiz Game
DB Team Trivia — Rules

Objective

Answer multiple-choice questions correctly and as fast as possible. The player with the most points at the end wins.

Setup

  • Everyone opens the game and enters their name to join the lobby.
  • One player clicks 👑 Become Host — only the host can start the game and control the pace.
  • The host picks a category (or Mixed), the number of questions, and the seconds per question.
  • The host can also add up to 50 custom questions using the 📋 Questions tab.
  • When everyone has joined, the host clicks 🎲 Load & Start Game.

Gameplay

  • Each question appears with 4 answer options (A, B, C, D) and a countdown timer.
  • Tap your answer before the timer runs out.
  • Speed matters — the faster you answer correctly, the more points you score (up to 1,000 per question).
  • A wrong answer scores zero — but you only get one attempt per question.
  • After time runs out, the host clicks Reveal Answers to show who got it right and their points.
  • The host then clicks Next Question → to continue.

Scoring

Points are speed-based. A correct answer the moment it appears scores close to 1,000 pts. Answering with 2 seconds left scores around 400 pts. Wrong answers score 0.

Winning

After all questions, the final leaderboard shows 🥇🥈🥉 medals and all player scores. The player with the most total points wins!

⏱️ How Long Will It Take?

Each question takes about 35–40 seconds in total — the answer timer plus the host revealing results and clicking Next. Use this to plan how many questions fit your session.

Word Game
DB Team Word Chain — Rules

Objective

Each round a word appears on screen. Type a valid word that follows two strict rules — the faster you answer, the more points you score.

The Two Rules

  • Your word must start with the last letter of the displayed word.
  • Your word must be exactly the same length as the displayed word.

Example: The word APPLE appears (5 letters, ends in E). A valid answer could be EAGLE, ELBOW, or EIGHT — all start with E and have 5 letters.

Setup

  • Everyone opens the game and enters their name to join the lobby.
  • One player clicks 👑 Become Host.
  • The host sets the number of rounds (3–20) and seconds per round (15–60).
  • The host chooses the word source — either built-in words or a custom word list (see below).
  • Host clicks Start Game.

Word Source Options

  • 🎲 Built-in Words — the game picks randomly from 200+ words each round. Words vary in length.
  • ✏️ My Custom Words — the host types their own words, one per line. Words can be any length and don't all have to be the same. Minimum 5 words required. Great for themed rounds — e.g. Oracle terms, Azure services, or anything relevant to your team.

Gameplay

  • A word appears and the round timer starts.
  • Type your answer in the box and press Enter or click →.
  • Wrong answers (wrong length or wrong starting letter) are rejected — you can keep trying until the timer runs out.
  • A live feed shows who has answered and what word they used.
  • The host can click End Round Early once everyone has answered.

Scoring

Up to 1,000 points for the fastest correct answer. The slower you are, the fewer points you earn. Only your first correct answer counts per round.

⏱️ How Long Will It Take?

Each round takes the timer duration plus ~10–15 seconds for the host to check the feed and click Next Round. Word Chain moves fast — it's the quickest of the three games.

Team Strategy
DB Team Code Names — Rules

Objective

Two teams — Red and Blue — race to find all their secret words on a 5×5 grid. The first team to find all their words wins. Hit the Assassin word and your team loses instantly.

Roles

  • 🕵️ Spymaster — can see all card colours. Gives one-word clues to guide their team. Cannot guess cards.
  • 👤 Operative — can only see the words, not the colours. Taps cards to guess based on the Spymaster's clue.
  • 👑 Host — configures the game settings and starts the game.

Setup

  • Everyone opens the game, enters their name, and picks Join Red or Join Blue. Teams must be balanced (max 1 player difference). You cannot change teams once chosen.
  • One player per team clicks 🕵️ Be Spy to become the Spymaster.
  • The host configures: words per team, max turns, max guesses per turn, clue timer, and guess timer.
  • Once both teams have a Spymaster, the host clicks Start Game.

Each Turn

  • The active Spymaster types one word + a number (e.g. Water… 3).
  • The word hints at that many cards on the board. The number tells operatives how many cards to look for.
  • Operatives discuss and tap cards they think match the clue.
  • They get up to the clue number + 1 bonus guess, capped by the host's max guesses setting.

What Each Guess Does

  • ✅ Your team's card — revealed in your colour, keep guessing (if guesses remain).
  • ⬜ Neutral card — revealed grey, your turn ends immediately.
  • 🔄 Other team's card — that card goes to them, your turn ends.
  • 💀 Assassin card — your team loses instantly!

Winning

  • First team to reveal all their words wins 🏆
  • If the Assassin is hit, the other team wins automatically.
  • If all turns run out, the team that found more words wins. A tie = 🤝 Draw!

⏱️ How Long Will It Take?

Codenames is the longest of the three games. Each turn has a clue phase and a guessing phase, and good teams take time to discuss. The host's turn and timer settings control the pace.

💡 Spymaster tip: Find a clue that links multiple of your cards without accidentally pointing at the Assassin or the other team's cards. The best Spymasters think several turns ahead.

Drawing Game
DB Doodle Dash — Rules

Objective

Red vs Blue team drawing-and-guessing battle. One player draws a secret word while their teammates — and the other team — race to guess it. Most points at the end wins!

Roles Each Round

  • ✏️ Drawer — sees the secret word and draws it on the canvas. Cannot write letters or numbers.
  • 👀 Guesser — types guesses into the chat box. Faster correct guesses = more points.
  • Roles rotate so everyone draws over the course of the game.

Setup

  • Everyone enters their name and joins the lobby.
  • Pick Join Red or Join Blue — teams must be balanced (max 1 difference).
  • One player clicks 👑 Become Host to configure and start.
  • Host sets: rounds, draw time, difficulty, wrong guess penalty, streak bonus, and word source.

Scoring

  • Correct guess — up to 1,000 pts based on speed. Later guessers score less.
  • Drawer — earns +50 pts each time someone guesses correctly.
  • Wrong guess penalty — optional deduction (25 or 50 pts) per wrong guess to discourage spam.
  • Streak bonus — if your team guesses correctly 3 or 5 rounds in a row, earn a big bonus!
  • Hint — the drawer can reveal one letter to help, but loses 100 pts.
  • High rating bonus — if a drawing averages 4+ stars, the drawer gets +100 pts.

After Each Round — Rate the Drawing!

Once the round ends, all guessers rate the drawing from 1–5 stars ⭐. High-rated drawers get bonus points. The best-rated drawing of the game earns a spot in the Hall of Fame!

Word Difficulties

🟢 Easy — DOG, CAKE, BIRD 🟡 Medium — VOLCANO, COMPASS 🔴 Hard — DATABASE, GOLDENGATE

Custom Words

The host can enter their own word list — one word per line. Add a difficulty tag with a colon: REPLICATION:hard, INDEX:easy. Great for Oracle/Azure themed rounds!

⏱️ How Long Will It Take?

Each round = draw time + ~20 seconds for rating. With rotating drawers, multiply by the number of players per team.
